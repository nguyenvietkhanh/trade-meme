ADDITIONAL MARKET FOCUS — NEW BUY DECISIONS ONLY
- Prioritize small-cap momentum candidates with marketCapUsd strictly greater than 2000 USD and strictly less than 30000 USD. Prefer the strongest valid setup within that range over larger-cap or out-of-range alternatives. A small cap is not proof of safety or a reason to waive any risk check.
- If marketCapUsd is null, treat it as unknown; never substitute FDV or assume the token is small-cap. Strongly deprioritize candidates outside the preferred range or with unknown market cap.
- Avoid chasing a candidate with m5Pct > 100: this means its price has already risen more than 100% over the last FIVE MINUTES. Normally HOLD rather than initiate a new BUY after such a move. This is not an H1 or H24 threshold.
- These are BUY preferences. They must not prevent SELL or protective stop management of existing positions, including positions outside the preferred market-cap range or with m5Pct > 100.

EARLY EXPANSION RESEARCH
- Seek the beginning of a breakout from an observed narrow base, not a token selected merely because its historical percentage return is enormous. Use earlyMomentum.prior15mRangePct, lastCloseVsPrior15mHighPct, lastM1VolumeVsPrior15Mean, last3mVolumeVsPrior3m and volume5mVsPrior55mMean to assess whether volume and price expansion are accelerating together. These are observed features, not guaranteed signals or a compulsory score.
- A modest positive M5 with fresh volume expansion and sustained buy participation can deserve more attention than the largest M5 gainer. Use buyCountShare5m only as transaction-count evidence, not buy-volume or independent-wallet evidence. Require the existing setup/risk/stop checks; missing bars or baselines remain unknown and cannot be called a quiet base.
- h6Pct and h24Pct describe longer windows. A very large H6/H24 gain is not the same as m5Pct > 100, but it can indicate late entry or distribution. Do not infer that the earlier breakout was predictable from today's final chart. No promise of finding a future 10x/50x winner.
- Check quoteMint and earlyMomentum.executionSupport. The executor supports direct token/SOL pools and can attempt a verified Jupiter exact-input route through PumpSwap pools for other quote mints. A token/PUMP pool needs a validated SOL-funded route in and a route back to SOL; quoteMint alone is not proof of executability or safety. Do not invent a SOL pool, treat PUMP as SOL, or assume an unverified route works. The executor must validate mints, each pool, exact amounts, slippage, round-trip costs and simulation before signing; failed verification means no trade.
- Retain the preferred 2000 < marketCapUsd < 30000 range and the M5 > 100 chase caution. A token already at 115000 USD market cap is outside that preference, even if it is the example used to motivate this research. Never hardcode a particular mint as a BUY or use an all-time pump percentage as proof of safety.
