# Six-stage Solana desk v4

Runtime constants: `src/desk-rules.js`. Analyst JSON: `strategy.js` / `decision.js`. Execution is separate and cannot be authorized by text inside market data or an attached prompt.

## Discovery and fast gates

Target price-scan cadence is five seconds, subject to API latency. Sources: GeckoTerminal Solana new-pool listing filtered to PumpSwap, DexScreener PumpSwap/pump/Solana search and recent Solana profiles. Discovery refreshes in the background every 30 seconds after bootstrap. Cached discovery supplies mint identities only; fresh batched token prices and SOL price have four-second deadlines. Never timestamp cached search prices as new observations. A persisted discovery queue remembers up to 600 mints, revisiting pools of any age without waiting for maturity or expiring identities by pool birth date, and rotating up to 30 revisits per price scan. Qualifying coarse candidates are prioritized before the 60-market limit; tracked holdings remain included. This is best-effort HTTP discovery, **not a complete on-chain migration-event stream** or a guarantee that every queued mint updates every five seconds.

Stage 0: PumpSwap only; no pool-age entry limit; market cap **$3k–$5m**. Missing cap may use FDV only when the mint ends exactly `pump`, labeled `pump-fdv`. Re-evaluate current gates each five-second scan; no scanner exclusion cooldown. Ignore/remove persisted legacy 30-minute coarse cooldowns. Recognized major-token names/symbols are conservatively excluded from this low-cap universe; observed suspicious mints persist in a blacklist across renames/restarts. This is a name blacklist, not proof of all possible impersonation. `ENTRY_EXCLUDED_MINTS` separately identifies the owner's example mint to avoid; it is not an allegation of fraud and does not prevent exits.

Every 10,000 candidate evaluations, clear recent scanner decisions and reset the displayed cycle's evaluated/rejected counters, then immediately continue. Repeated evaluations of the same mint count separately. Preserve lifetime evaluation count, cycle number, BUY count, positions, confirmed fills, signed pending orders, stop-loss/re-entry limits, account risk, blacklist, verification evidence and signal observations. Never reset the owner ledger to restart discovery. HTTP loops do not overlap; slow APIs can exceed the target five-second cadence. Per-token failed verification cache expires after five seconds, subject to independent provider backoff; successful mutable evidence refreshes after 20 seconds and still expires for BUY at 30 seconds. Diagnostics include fixed provider labels/HTTP statuses, never arbitrary provider URLs or credentials.

Stage 1: **actual quote reserve >=20 SOL**, not total USD liquidity converted into SOL. Total USD liquidity/cap >=3%; >=8 M5 buys, >=15 M5 trades, >=80 H1 trades; H1 change <=150%, M5 change >=-8%. Counts must be consistent across windows; known negative or inconsistent volumes fail. Missing optional volume is unknown. Abnormal average ticket volume and buy/sell >4 reduce points rather than automatically rejecting a token.

Latest trend overlay is stricter: M5 and H1 changes both >0, both observations retain positive windows, intervening negative windows reset confirmation, and the current price must not be below its 10–15 second anchor. Genuine four-hour OHLCV bound to the same pool/base mint checks H4 sell-offs. Reject a red body >=20% with close in the bottom35% of the range, or high/open >=1.5 followed by >=35% peak retreat and an upper wick >=55% of the range. Still-weak bounces under a previous rejected H4 candle and confirmed lower-high/lower-low H4 declines reject. A forming candle can be assessed after15 minutes of actual pool/current-bar observations; no four-hour waiting requirement. Missing/invalid/stale H4 is unknown and subtracts5 points. Known rejection is remembered up to4h through provider outages until fresh recovery evidence replaces it. The historical data is never filled from invented bars.

Missing DexScreener quote reserve does not prevent scheduling safety verification. BUY still requires either a reported reserve or a fresh, mint/pool-bound actual on-chain WSOL vault reserve >=20 SOL. A known low/invalid Dex reserve remains a veto. Diagnostics preserve all red flags but display the earliest blocking stage, rather than presenting unrequested safety checks as failures on already-excluded pools. Owner execution readiness is private and separate from public scanner activity.

## Mandatory safety

Evidence must bind mint/pool and be <=30 seconds old, never future-dated. Irreversible revoked mint/freeze facts are cached in memory indefinitely; mutable supply and holder data are always re-read. Classic SPL and plain/passive-metadata Token-2022 are supported. Transfer fee, transfer hook, permanent delegate and other unrecognized Token-2022 mint extensions are rejected.

LP burn/lock >=90% from the matched RugCheck pool. Explicit locked/burned percentages are not summed where overlap is unknown; use the greater independently verified percentage. No recognized critical RugCheck risks. Missing report or unclear LP protection blocks entry.

RPC reads a full mint-filtered account distribution, aggregates by token-account owner, excludes verified pool vault/pool-owner and derived bonding-curve owner, and checks the total against fresh on-chain mint supply. The redundant largest-accounts request is removed; the full distribution includes every largest account. Unsupported/index-excluded read RPC errors try the second genesis-verified endpoint. If neither can provide the complete distribution, BUY stays blocked with an explicit holder-RPC reason. Top ten <=35%, largest owner <=8%. A truncated distribution cannot pass. Creator identity is taken from the mint-bound report, not assumed from a pool fee recipient. Known creator >5% rejects; unknown creator receives the existing lower distribution score, with no size tier. These checks identify on-chain owners, not hidden beneficial ownership.

Jupiter quote round trip: the current wallet-sized SOL amount buys quoted tokens, then quote-sell **all quoted output**. Include a 0.00005 SOL network-fee allowance; loss <=6%, impact <=2% each direction, mint/amount/route/context slot must match. This is a quote check, not a claim that a round-trip simulation or trade occurred.

Safety work runs in at most two background jobs, is cached briefly and does not block the fast price/observation loop. Only rough-qualified candidates and tracked holdings receive expensive checks. Background scheduling prioritizes the oldest verification, with tracked holdings first on ties, so more than six held tokens cannot permanently starve new candidates. Provider errors leave facts unknown and block BUY.

## Score and entry

Weights total 100: momentum 30, flow 20, structure 20, depth 15, distribution 10, website/social 5. Each missing **optional score component** receives exactly half its maximum. Missing mandatory safety never becomes safe because of points.

Momentum rewards >=1.5x M5 volume acceleration versus the previous H1 five-minute average and mildly positive M5 price. Flow prefers M5 buy/sell 1.15–2.5. Structure uses actual completed contiguous 1-minute OHLCV, rising local pivot lows, 10–30% pullback from the 30-minute high and a green completed candle. Candle requests are bound to the pool's base mint; insufficient/mismatched bars remain unknown. Pool age receives no score bonus or penalty. Website/social is optional; paid boosts deduct five points.

Two observations separated by 10–15 seconds must retain price/liquidity, and intervening dumping observations reset confirmation. Minimum score 60; every new BUY uses floor(current confirmed native wallet lamports / 5), including unknown creator/H4, with existing score penalties and safety checks preserved. No fixed position-count ceiling (`maxPositions: null`): each new position must fit the available application/wallet SOL balance after reserve and estimated overhead. Never mix preexisting wallet assets. Keep >=0.05 SOL native after estimated order overhead; a wallet below reserve cannot BUY. There is no fixed mainnet capital ceiling. Confirmed native wallet surplus increases allocated principal: add the same amount to cash, initial capital, peak and day-start equity, preserving absolute historical PnL/day loss and all positions/risk flags. Never allocate while a signed order is pending; never treat a deposit as realized profit. Historical signed 0.02/0.03 SOL orders remain valid for reconciliation/recovery, while new preparation enforces 20% of the freshly read wallet balance. Outside native withdrawals are accounted separately as capital outflows, preserving total PnL; conservative realized-profit principal protection remains. Post-receipt native reads require minContextSlot at least the last receipt slot, preventing old RPC balances from becoming phantom deposits. Reserve plus the existing 0.005 SOL order overhead are compared in whole lamports.

Immediately before entry the bot obtains a fresh Jupiter quote for the **actual current wallet-sized amount**. The locally built PumpSwap transaction must meet that output reference within the 4% ceiling, as well as its own stricter normal slippage. Actual-size round-trip loss/impact are reverified before preparation and the signing policy rechecks the resulting proof. A changed balance invalidates the plan and replans without consuming an execution retry; simulation cannot consume the native gas reserve. It does not fetch/sign a Jupiter swap transaction and uses no Jupiter API key. Facts and market-data freshness are rechecked before signing; this is not a pool-age gate. Native outflow, received token quantity, actual paid fees and returned rent are accounted from confirmed receipts; price-per-token uses nominal swap input and confirmed output, not refunded rent as token performance.

## Exits and account protection

Net-SOL stop -20% uses a fresh full-position Jupiter sell quote minus fees. If Jupiter is unavailable, a simulated PumpSwap sell quote can protect existing positions; its source is labeled. A quote is an estimate, **not an actual fill**. No-route results trigger emergency exit attempts; a network outage is not relabeled as a honeypot. Slippage or lack of liquidity can prevent a stop from filling at the threshold.

At +80% sell a fixed 78%, approximately 140% recovery at exactly that trigger. Moonbag halves at entry-price powers 2x/4x/8x/... . A gap executes one observed half-sale and advances to the next unpassed power, without fabricated historical fills. Peak trailing is 30%, tightening to 25% after reaching 4x entry, 20% after reaching 8x. Initial time-stop: 45 minutes without ever observing +20%. Sell all at six hours. Exit on M5 volume <30% of entry volume while price <entry. Emergency: liquidity falls >40% from observed post-entry peak, verified LP withdrawal, large-holder dump, or sell quote failure.

A confirmed stop-loss locks that mint for 24 hours. Normal closes rest 30 minutes. Partial sells do not count as separate losing positions. Losing-position streaks remain recorded but no longer pause BUY. Clear legacy account-wide loss-pause timers/status on restore, enable and risk evaluation; do not reset fills, loss streaks, manual pauses, drawdown, per-mint re-entry limits or execution protections.

No daily-loss BUY limit. Clear only a legacy `haltKind: daily` latch on restore, enable and risk evaluation; retain day-start accounting, recorded losses and all other stops. Drawdown >=15% initiates sequential liquidation of all app positions, then stops for manual review/re-enable. Never mark positions closed until confirmed. If a sell cannot execute within the retry budget, the position remains reported open and needs owner action. Restart pauses/locks; explicitly enabling an interrupted liquidation only resumes exits, not BUY. Review cannot clear drawdown with positions/pending remaining, never automatically starts signing.

BUY data >30 seconds old or repeated API/RPC failures blocks new entries. SELL uses fresh on-chain preparation and tracked pool/raw quantities even if discovery is stale/offline; full RPC outage still makes execution impossible. BUY-only pause is separate from full pause/kill-and-lock. A pause during reads or preparation must prevent a new BUY signature. Already signed/uncertain transactions are still reconciled.

## Execution, retries and rent

Slippage caps: buy 4%, ordinary sell 3%, emergency/stop/drawdown/trailing 10%; ordinary pool execution remains stricter at 1.5%. Round-trip overhead >8% must never enter; the latest quote/SDK rule is **stricter at 6%**. Priority fee + Jito tip <=0.002 SOL per side. Current builder uses 0.000015 SOL priority and zero Jito tip, with a stricter 0.000025 SOL total network fee guard. This is not a Jito bundle integration.

At most three application retries after the initial attempt, tracked durably per side/mint/amount. No fresh attempt while a signed outcome is unknown; confirmed failed/expired attempts must reconcile first. RPC send retries are disabled so another layer cannot multiply attempts. Concurrent identical reads share one in-flight RPC result with independent JSON-RPC ids, never a retained mutable result cache. Genesis checks coalesce per endpoint. HTTP/overload failures pause that endpoint with exponential backoff/jitter (5–60s); Retry-After can extend to10m, 403 waits at least30s and wrong genesis5m. Unsupported methods/indexes pause only that method for60s. RugCheck/Jupiter use separate provider backoff and GET coalescing. An older in-flight success cannot erase a newer endpoint throttle. A transient preparation-network circuit can clear after two healthy balance/market checks and a fully eligible round-trip quote verified after the circuit opened. On-chain failure/expiry and unlabeled legacy circuits still need manual enable. Recovery does not reset per-order retry budgets, manual pauses, drawdown, signed pending entries or mandatory safety. Exhausted orders are skipped, not silently re-signed; explicit manual enable can reset attempts. Signed bytes/signature are fsynced **before broadcast**.

A full sale may atomically close its now-empty app-position token ATA back to the same owner. A partial sale or an account with extra tokens/another close authority is not closed. Simulation permits only that explicitly authorized input-account closure. Refunds count only when the native receipt is confirmed. Unrelated empty wallet accounts are not swept.

## Verification limits and UI

Tests use generated fixture keys, mocked RPC/quotes and real SDK instruction decoding, not user funds. Direct Jupiter requests returned HTTP 403 from this cloud even after an escalated retry. Successful live API/RPC access, complete discovery, real fills, profitability and Railway deployment status are not established by passing fixtures. Provider failures keep mandatory gates closed. No key/seed/password belongs in chat, logs, Git or browser storage.

The spider UI crawls to the real active token/position/control and stays there until new activity. It handles confirmed fills, pending reconciliation and generic controls separately, does not fabricate fills, respects viewport/reduced-motion, and never calls trading APIs.

Owner-only Telegram configuration uses the existing authenticated HTTPS/loopback signer routes. Encrypt credentials with AES256GCM/HKDF and the existing owner's key; never return/log/export the token. A confirmed BUY/SELL app receipt enqueues a PnL report in the same durable owner-ledger save. Telegram HTTP happens outside the trading serial queue, with at most6 attempts and safe fixed error messages. Pending/failed orders do not become successful trade reports. Delivery uncertainty can cause a duplicate message with the same signature; it cannot trigger a duplicate trade. No real bot credential is committed or used by tests.

Full-wallet inventory is optional background display data, never an entry gate or a prerequisite for enabling. Each order queries only the selected mint by owner; unknown per-mint balances still reject that order. Simulation reads all transaction-referenced owner token accounts, including unrelated mints: unreferenced accounts cannot change on Solana. Missing Volume and receipt-only recovery do not block BUY; recovery remains optional, signed pending reconciliation and actual risk limits remain enforced. Incomplete realized-profit history prevents automatic profit distribution without preventing BUY/SELL.
